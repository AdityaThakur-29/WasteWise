# PRD — WasteWise
## Data Driven Waste Management Awareness and Insights Platform

**Project Type:** College Field Project  
**Domain:** Waste Management / Data Analysis / Public Awareness  
**Primary Topic:** Waste Management Data Collection & Analysis  
**Project Description:** Survey waste segregation and collection; propose optimization plans or awareness campaigns.

---

## 1. Product Overview

**WasteWise** is a data driven waste management awareness and insights website developed as part of a college field project.

The platform presents findings from a real world waste management survey conducted through Google Forms. Instead of being only a generic environmental awareness website, it transforms collected survey responses into understandable statistics, charts, key findings, optimization suggestions, and practical waste management awareness content.

### Core Flow

**Collect Data → Analyze Data → Identify Problems → Propose Improvements → Educate Users**

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Waste management problems are often discussed generally, but local waste management practices can vary significantly between communities.

Common problems may include:

- Inconsistent waste segregation
- Lack of separate waste bins
- Limited awareness of proper disposal
- Irregular waste collection
- Mixing recyclable and non recyclable waste
- Improper e waste disposal
- Limited access to recycling facilities

The project aims to understand these issues through actual survey data and communicate the findings through an interactive website.

# 17. Optimization Plan

This section directly addresses the project description:

> Propose optimization plans or awareness campaigns.

Use the following structure:

```text
IDENTIFIED PROBLEM
        ↓
EVIDENCE FROM SURVEY
        ↓
PROPOSED SOLUTION
        ↓
EXPECTED IMPACT
```

## Example

### Problem

Low availability of separate bins.

### Evidence

A significant number of respondents report not having separate bins.

### Proposed Solution

Introduce clearly labelled wet and dry waste bins at appropriate collection points.

### Expected Impact

Make segregation easier and reduce mixing of waste.

Recommendations must be connected to actual survey findings.

# 21. Do's and Don'ts

## Do

- Separate wet and dry waste.
- Keep recyclable materials reasonably clean and dry.
- Use designated collection systems.
- Dispose of e waste through appropriate channels.
- Reduce unnecessary single use products.

## Don't

- Mix wet and dry waste unnecessarily.
- Throw batteries into general waste.
- Dump waste in open areas.
- Burn household waste.
- Put everything into one bin.

# 31. Data Integrity Rules

This is critical for an academic field project.

The website must:

- Never invent survey statistics.
- Never alter results to make them look better.
- Clearly distinguish survey findings from general information.
- Show the sample size.
- State that findings represent survey respondents and may not represent the entire population.
- Calculate percentages from actual responses.
- Handle missing responses correctly.
- Avoid misleading visualizations.
- Clearly label examples or placeholder data during development.
